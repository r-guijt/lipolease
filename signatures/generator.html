How to Use

Easy Paste (Recommended)

Click the Copy Signature (Rich Text) button. Open your email program (Outlook, Gmail, or Apple Mail) settings, create a new signature, click inside the editor, and press Cmd + V (Mac) or Ctrl + V (Windows).

Gmail (Web)

Go to Settings > See all settings > General > Signature. Paste the copied signature, check "Insert signature before quoted text", and save changes.

Outlook (Desktop App)

Go to Preferences > Signatures (Mac) or File > Options > Mail > Signatures (Windows). Paste inside the text editor and save.
